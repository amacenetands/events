Sadler’s Wells Culture Club encourages you to discover new passions (or develop existing ones) through dance and the performing arts.

There are talks and workshops that explore creativity, wellbeing, dance and the artistic programme at Sadler’s Wells.

We meet from 2 pm – 3 pm on Fridays. It’s open to anyone 18+, no matter what your ability or experience may be.

There will be a limited number of tickets available on the day, offered on a first come first serve basis. We recommend arriving early to avoid disappointment. Please get in touch with learning@sadlerswells.com if you have any questions about Culture Club.

October workshops

English National Ballet Workshop

Friday 2 October

English National Ballet are bringing Rhythm Riot to our stage and Culture Club. This workshop will be a talk giving insight into the production.

Far From Home Movement Workshop

Friday 9 October

Ahead of their show at Sadler’s Well’s East, Alleyne Dance Company will invite Culture Club members to discover the movement language of their show.

Ballet Black Workshop

Friday 16 October

Dancer Bhungane Mehlomakulu will join Culture Club to deliver a Ballet workshop.

Dance Floor Taster: Funky Social Dance

Friday 23 October

We will be hosting a Funky Social dance workshop led by Isaac Baptiste.

November workshops

Louise Klarnett Wellbeing Workshop

Friday 6 November

Louise will lead a dance and movement session drawing on imagination, playfulness and curiosity. A gentle, sensory practice that invites connection, creativity and a deeper sense of wellbeing through listening to and exploring the body.

Dance Floor Taster: Afro-Caribbean Fusion

Friday 13 November

Join Miguel Gonzalez in an exploration of Afro-Cuban dance styles.

Movement workshop with Akeim Toussaint

Friday 20 November

Founder of Dance Company Toussaint To Move, Akeim is an interdisciplinary performer and maker, born in Jamaica and raised in England. Akeim’s movement interest has a myriad of inspirations that include Capoeira, Martial Arts, Contemporary Dance, Contact Improvisation, Caribbean Dance, Hip Hop and Yoga.
