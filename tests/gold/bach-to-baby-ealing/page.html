About the concerts

Bach to Baby is the critically acclaimed live music concert series for babies, toddlers and their carers, delighting families since 2011. Tots dance, wriggle and revel in the wonder of live music by your side, while you take a moment out of your busy day to hear top notch performances by outstanding musicians, with your young babes in tow. Feeds, naps and nappy changes are all part of the fun.

Arrive early to ditch the buggy, find your favourite spot and catch up with friends and family before the music begins.

For further questions, check out our FAQ.

How to get there

Haven Green Baptist Church, 2 Castlebar Road, Ealing Broadway, London, W5 2UP

Public Transport:
Closest stations: Ealing Broadway.
Bus Routes: The E10 from Northolt and the 297 from Wilesden both stop just around the corner from the church.

Parking:
There is limited car parking at the church.

Accessibility: The venue is fully accessible.

About the venue

Haven Green Baptist Church, built in 1881, stands just across from Ealing Broadway station and offers a light, welcoming setting for Bach to Baby family concerts. Families can enjoy live music, then explore Ealing's bustling shops and cafés or relax on Haven Green itself. With its deep community roots and excellent transport connections, Haven Green makes an ideal West London venue for cultural outings with little ones.
